Visiting Pandharpur

Vitthal and Rukmini, the Chandrabhaga, and the temple’s published daily programme.

Bhakti Marg, Sangola Naka, Pandharpur, Maharashtra 413304

Pandharpur, in Solapur district, is the town of Lord Vitthal and Goddess Rukmini. The temple stands by the Chandrabhaga river. Ashadhi Ekadashi and Kartiki Ekadashi bring the largest wari crowds, so rooms should be requested early for those dates.

A day at the temple

The Shri Vitthal Rukmini Mandir Samiti publishes this daily programme. Confirm it on vitthalrukminimandir.org before you go, because the schedule can change on festival days.

Kakad Aarti and Nitya Pooja4:00 AM – 5:30 AM
Mahanaivedya10:45 AM – 11:00 AM
Poshakh4:40 PM – 5:00 PM
Dhoop Aarti6:45 PM – 7:00 PM
Shej Aarti12:00 AM – 1:00 AM

Chandrabhaga

Many devotees visit the river ghat as part of the yatra. Ask reception for the simplest way from Sangola Naka.

Meals

Pandharpur has many pure vegetarian bhojanalayas near the temple. Reception can point you to ones open during your stay.

Your room

After darshan, the stay is back at Bhakti Marg. Book the room on WhatsApp +91 77709 03081. Darshan passes are separate, on the temple website.
