A devotee stay beside the yatra

Shri Vitthal Rukmini Bhakta Niwas is a lodging at Bhakti Marg, Sangola Naka, Pandharpur, Maharashtra 413304. Devotees stay here while they visit Shri Vitthal Rukmini Temple, on the bank of the Chandrabhaga.

The rooms are the four types listed on this site: AC Deluxe at ₹1,500, Non-AC at ₹1,000, a Non-AC 6-bed room at ₹2,000 for the room, and Family Deluxe at ₹2,500. Call or WhatsApp +91 77709 03081 with your dates and the number of guests.

Where it is

Bhakti Marg, Sangola Naka. Auto and taxi drivers in Pandharpur know this address. It is a short ride from the temple, the railway station and the bus stand.

Who it is for

Families, couples and small yatri groups who want a clean room and a direct number for reception. Carry a government photo ID for every adult.

Darshan passes

Temple darshan passes are booked on the temple committee site, vitthalrukminimandir.org. This page is for the room only.
